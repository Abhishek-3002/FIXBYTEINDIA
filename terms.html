CMADD Connected Medical Partner Program — Terms

This program is administered by FIXBYTE INDIA in association with Choudhary Medical & Drug Distributors (CMADD).

Eligibility

A medical store earns one ₹99 static website offer when valid CMADD purchase bills total at least ₹12,000 within one four-consecutive-calendar-day cycle. The cycle starts from the first eligible sale bill date. Paid, credit and partial-payment bills count.

Returns and cancelled bills

Cancelled bills do not count. A return must reference its original sale bill and is deducted from that original cycle. If a return reduces an already qualified cycle below ₹12,000, the reward is marked for review. FIXBYTE may request supporting bill records before confirming the offer.

Repeat offers

Each separate qualifying cycle can earn one additional offer. One permanent partner card is issued per store; new rewards are linked to the same card. Extra purchases above the ₹12,000 threshold do not roll forward to another cycle.

Partner connection

CMADD connection status is manually maintained by FIXBYTE based on the business relationship confirmation available to it. An inactive connection prevents claiming an offer until reactivated. Card identity and historical records may remain visible for verification.

Card and QR

Cards are issued by FIXBYTE INDIA. A card is valid only when the card status and CMADD connection both show active on the official verification portal. Screenshots or printed copies do not override live portal status.

Website scope

The ₹99 offer covers one static website with scope and delivery details confirmed by FIXBYTE before work begins. Domain, hosting, paid third-party services, additional pages, advanced features and ongoing maintenance are not included unless expressly agreed in writing.

Program changes

FIXBYTE may correct records, investigate suspected misuse and update program operations. Material changes will be reflected in the official program website.
