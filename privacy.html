Privacy Notice

FIXBYTE INDIA operates the CMADD Connected Medical Partner verification platform.

Information handled

The platform may store medical-store name, owner name, business contact details, address, optional GSTIN, purchase bill references, bill dates and amounts, partner-card status, reward records and website-order details.

Why it is used

Information is used to maintain partner records, calculate the four-day ₹12,000 eligibility rule, issue and verify partner cards, manage ₹99 website offers, prevent duplicate bill entries and maintain an audit trail.

Public QR verification

Public verification shows only limited information such as store name, card ID, card status, CMADD connection status and available offer count. It does not display bills, mobile numbers, email addresses or GSTIN.

Access and security

Administrative access is restricted to authorized FIXBYTE personnel. Database credentials are kept server-side. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online system can be guaranteed completely risk-free.

Retention and correction

Partner, bill and audit records are retained for business verification and dispute handling. Contact FIXBYTE INDIA to request correction of inaccurate information. Records are not deleted through the admin dashboard.
