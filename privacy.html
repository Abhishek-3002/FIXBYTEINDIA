YOUR DATA · YOUR CHOICES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how FIXBYTE INDIA (“FIXBYTE”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit our website, enquire about services, request a meeting, submit a review or complaint, create a join ID, or make a payment.

Who to contact

For privacy questions, data-access/correction/deletion requests, or complaints, contact fixbytesupports@gmail.com. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant record. Do not email passwords, OTPs, full card details or other sensitive credentials.

1. Personal data we may collect

  • Contact and identity details you submit, such as name, phone number, email address and business name.
  • Service information, including selected plan, project requirements, meeting preferences, messages, support requests, reviews and complaints.
  • Order and payment-related details, such as plan, amount, payment status and transaction/reference ID. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider; we do not ask you to send card PINs, CVVs, passwords or OTPs to us.
  • Technical information that may be generated when you use the site, such as IP/device/browser information, timestamps, diagnostic logs and security events, depending on hosting and configuration.

2. Why we use it

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, manage projects and meetings, process and verify payments, provide support, handle complaints and refunds, maintain records, protect the website, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and communicate about a service you requested. We will not use personal data for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate lawful basis or required notice/consent.

3. Consent and your choices

Where consent is the basis for processing, we aim to explain the relevant purpose clearly and request an affirmative choice. You can withdraw consent by contacting us at the email above; withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful and may affect a service that depends on the data. Service-related messages and optional promotional messages are different; we will seek a separate choice where required for marketing.

4. When information may be shared

We may share only relevant information with service providers needed to operate the service, such as website hosting/deployment, database, payment processing, email or WhatsApp communication providers, as applicable to the feature you use. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, our rights, and service security. Providers may process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not promise that every third-party service is used on every visit.

5. Payments

Payment processing and verification may be handled through a payment provider (including Razorpay if enabled for the transaction). Payment availability, test/live mode and the provider shown at checkout determine the applicable flow. Check the provider's own privacy terms before paying. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or passwords with FIXBYTE support.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including project delivery, support, accounting, dispute resolution, security and applicable legal requirements. Some records may need to be retained where law requires it. You may request correction or deletion; we will review the request and explain if a lawful or operational reason requires limited retention.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service, such as access restrictions and safeguards for systems under our control. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a unique password, protect your email and device, and report suspected misuse promptly.

8. Children

Our services are intended for people able to enter into service arrangements. If a feature is directed to or knowingly collects personal data from a child, applicable child-data and parental-consent requirements must be followed. Please contact us if you believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate authorisation.

9. Third-party links and services

Our site may link to WhatsApp, payment services, social platforms or other third-party sites. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies, not this policy. Review those policies before using the services.

10. Your requests and complaints

You can contact us to ask about the personal data we hold, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting. We aim to respond within a reasonable time and any applicable legal deadline. You may also have rights or remedies under applicable Indian law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, systems or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Please check it periodically.
