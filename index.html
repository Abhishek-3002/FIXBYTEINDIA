How the partner program works

One FIXBYTE-administered system manages store records, purchase bills, eligibility, partner cards and website orders.

01

Store Registration

Your medical store is registered in the FIXBYTE partner system with basic business details.

02

Four-Day Purchase

Valid CMADD bills totalling ₹12,000 within one four-calendar-day cycle qualify for one offer. Paid and credit bills count.

03

Partner Card

FIXBYTE INDIA issues one permanent card with a unique QR code after the first confirmed eligibility.

04

Verify & Claim

Scan the card to check authenticity, current CMADD connection and available website offers.

Eligibility rules

Earn the offer

  • ₹12,000 total valid purchases within four consecutive calendar days
  • Paid and credit bills both count
  • Returns reduce the qualifying total; cancelled bills do not count
  • Each bill number can be recorded only once per store
  • Every new qualifying cycle earns one additional ₹99 offer
  • CMADD connection must be Active when the offer is claimed
One permanent partner card is issued. New qualifying cycles add new website rewards to the same card. Extra purchases above ₹12,000 do not roll into the next cycle.

Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Who issues the partner card?

FIXBYTE INDIA issues and manages the permanent partner card. CMADD does not need a separate dashboard.

Who enters purchase bills?

FIXBYTE Admin manually records CMADD bill details. Paid and credit bills count when valid.

Can I earn the ₹99 offer more than once?

Yes. Every separate four-day cycle that reaches ₹12,000 can earn one additional website offer.

What if my CMADD connection becomes inactive?

Your card remains identifiable, but the verification page shows that the connection is inactive. The offer cannot be claimed until reactivated.
